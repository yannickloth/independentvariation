Published Papers

The published papers behind the site. Each one carries the full formal development: definitions, theorems, and proofs.

The site explains results and applies them; it does not prove them. Every formal result used on the site is developed in full — definitions, theorems, and proofs — in a published paper on Zenodo. This page lists those papers, with what each one contains and its DOI.
